About this course

Scientific English will help learners develop the language needed to read scientific literature, understand research abstracts, describe data, explain findings and participate in scientific discussions.

Planned topics include scientific vocabulary, research articles, abstracts, describing methods and results, presenting findings and communicating scientific ideas to specialist and general audiences.

Further course details and enrolment information will be added as the course is developed.
