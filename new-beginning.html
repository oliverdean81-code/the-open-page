Start learning: New Zealand

These short beginner lessons introduce useful English expressions. Open a lesson to read and practise the phrases aloud.

1. First conversations

Introductions and asking for help with English.

Open lesson
  • Hello, my name is … — Introduce yourself.
  • I'm new to New Zealand. — Explain that you have recently arrived.
  • Could you speak more slowly, please? — Ask someone to slow down.
  • Could you repeat that, please? — Ask to hear something again.
  • I'm still learning English. — Explain your language needs.

Try it: Say your name, then ask someone to speak more slowly.

2. Shopping for essentials

Finding food, asking prices and paying.

Open lesson
  • How much does this cost? — Ask the price.
  • Where can I find bread? — Ask where something is.
  • Do you have a smaller size? — Ask about sizes.
  • Can I pay by card? — Ask about payment.
  • Could I have a receipt, please? — Ask for proof of purchase.

Try it: Imagine you are in a supermarket. Ask where to find milk and how much it costs.

3. Visiting a doctor

Making appointments and communicating your needs.

Open lesson
  • I'd like to make an appointment. — Ask to see a health professional.
  • I don't feel well. — Say that you are unwell.
  • I have a headache. — Describe a symptom.
  • Is an interpreter available? — Ask for language assistance.
  • Could you explain that again, please? — Ask for clarification.

Try it: Practise asking for an appointment and an interpreter.

These expressions are for language practice, not medical advice.

More lessons are planned: housing, transport, employment, schools, banking, important documents, emergencies, friendships and finding support. Country-specific service information will be added after checking reliable local sources.

Learning with dignity

People arriving in a new country bring many languages, skills and experiences. These resources focus on the practical English needed to navigate unfamiliar situations, without making assumptions about anyone's education or background.

We hope to add carefully reviewed translations, listening practice and printable vocabulary sheets as this free programme develops.
