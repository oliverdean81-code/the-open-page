Academic English

Think critically. Write confidently. Communicate academically.

About this course

Academic English is designed for upper-intermediate and advanced learners who want to develop the language and analytical skills needed for university study and research.

Students will practise reading critically, evaluating evidence, constructing arguments and communicating ideas in clear, well-organised academic English.

Target level: B2–C1. Preparatory material for B1 learners may be added as the course develops.

Planned course modules

The course is planned as ten modules. Readings, video lectures, activities and assessments will be added as the content is prepared.

01. Understanding Academic Language
02. Critical Reading and Analysis
03. Academic Vocabulary
04. Constructing Arguments
05. Writing Academic Paragraphs
06. Essay Writing
07. Research and Referencing
08. Presenting Academic Ideas
09. Writing Research Summaries
10. Final Academic Writing Project

How you will learn

Each module is planned to include a video lecture, substantial reading, academic vocabulary, comprehension and critical-thinking questions, practical writing tasks and suggested answers. Teacher feedback will support students as they develop their own academic writing.

Course fees, enrolment and certificate details will be published when the course is ready.
