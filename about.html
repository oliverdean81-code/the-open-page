About the Teacher

Welcome to The Open Page.

With almost 20 years of teaching experience and a Master's degree in Translation and Creative Writing, I have a deep passion for language, literature and storytelling.

I created The Open Page to help learners develop their English, explore literature and poetry, engage with current affairs, and express their ideas with confidence.

I believe that reading opens doors to new perspectives, encourages curiosity and helps us understand the world around us.
