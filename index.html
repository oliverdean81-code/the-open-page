The Open Page – English Through Literature

The Open Page helps adults improve their English through literature, poetry and news. You can read in English, build your vocabulary, practise reading comprehension and explore ideas in greater depth.

  • Read short stories, poems and news articles in English.
  • Learn useful vocabulary and expressions.
  • Develop reading comprehension and critical-thinking skills.
  • Watch regular video lessons presented by a native English speaker.
  • Choose learning materials at B1 (Intermediate), B2 (Upper Intermediate) or C1 (Advanced) level.

The explanations on this page are available in your language. The learning materials are in English so you can practise and develop your skills.
