Welcome to The Open Page

A little about your teacher

I am an experienced teacher with almost 20 years of teaching experience and a Master's degree in Translation and Creative Writing.

Throughout my teaching career, I have developed a passion for language, literature and the power of words to inspire, challenge and connect us.

The Open Page brings together my interests in teaching, storytelling, poetry and current affairs. Through carefully selected texts, engaging video lectures and thoughtful learning activities, I hope to help students develop their English language skills, deepen their understanding of literature and discover the confidence to express themselves.

Every story opens a door. Every poem offers a new perspective. Every page is an opportunity to learn.
